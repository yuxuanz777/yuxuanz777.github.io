1. 一次出手的完整流程

  1. 持球玩家只在 Weak 阶段出手。不在 Weak 时持球等待，下一次 Weak 开始后经过「进入 Weak 后出手反应」再投。
  2. 球飞向 Boss，按命中率判定。未命中则球落地变成 Idle：连击清零，经过「捡球耗时」后由另一名玩家继续。
  3. 命中时按命中那一刻的 Boss 状态结算伤害，Boss 扣血。
  4. 球自动回弹给另一名玩家（RedirectToTeammate）。回弹在物理上没进接球圈就落地，或按接球率没接住，都算掉球，连击清零。
  5. 接住时连击 +1，接球后冷却 0.75 s；下一次出手等待 max(冷却, 瞄准耗时)。

2. 伤害

Weak 中：伤害 = BaseOrbDamage × (1 + DamageRatioPerRebound × min(n, nMaxRebound)) × (1 + 每轮加伤 × (k − 1)) 非 Weak：伤害 = 非 Weak 伤害（当前 1，不吃连击和轮次加成） HP = Clamp(HP − 伤害, 0, MaxHP)；HP ≤ 0 时 Boss 死亡

n 是连击数（nReboundCount），k 是第几轮 Weak。勾选「第 1 轮也加成」时公式里的 (k − 1) 改为 k。

来源：MyOrbActor::GetFinalOrbDamage；BP_Enemy「HP Damage」事件（EnemyState == Weakened 时取 FinalDamage，否则取 1.0）。每轮加伤是规划中的参数，游戏里还没有。

3. 连击数 n 与回弹计数 R

  • n：每接住一次 +1（普通接和 Perfect 接相同），上限 nMaxRebound。
  • R：每次打中 Boss 先 +1 再算回弹速度，没有上限（只受最大速度限制）。
  • 两者只在球进入 Idle 状态时清零（SetOrbState(Idle) → ResetRebound）。会进入 Idle 的情况：球在地上停住（没打中 / 没接住）、接球拉回途中接球人失效、球出界重生、玩家掉落重生时带着的球被传送、团灭或读档重置。
  • Weak 结束、持球时长、不打 Boss 直接互传、玩家受伤，都不会清零。所以只在 Weak 出手的策略下，Weak 结束时手里有球，连击会保留到下一轮。

来源：DreamCatchersCharacter::ExecuteCatch → IncrementRebound；MyOrbActor::HandleStateChange 的 Idle 分支；OnProjectileStop、TeleportToRespawn。

4. 投掷飞行时间

投速 v = 基础速度 × (1 + ThrowSpeedRatioPerRebound × n)，不超过 BaseMaxSpeed 快速投掷：基础速度 = BaseThrowSpeed，重力缩放 = BaseGravityScale 瞄准投掷：基础速度 = Lerp(AimThrowSpeed, AimThrowSpeedMax, 蓄力)，重力缩放 = Lerp(AimThrowGravityScale, AimGravityScaleMax, 蓄力) 飞行时间 = d / (v · cos θ)，θ 为能命中 (d, 受击点高度) 的低弧解

游戏里的投掷不追踪目标（代码注释写明自动瞄准未实现，Target 恒为空），出手仰角 = 准星方向 + atan(ThrowArcZ)。模拟器假设玩家会把准星压到刚好命中的角度，所以直接用抛体低弧解。超出最大射程时页面会提示，并按 45° 估算飞行时间。

5. 回弹飞行（会随 R 加速）

回弹速度 = BaseThrowSpeed × 0.7 + R × 100，不超过 BaseMaxSpeed 仰角 = max(指向队友的仰角, MinimumLaunchPitchDegrees) 重力 = 980 × 本次投掷的重力缩放（回弹沿用投掷时的重力）

从 Boss 受击点出发做逐帧积分（1/240 s），直到球与接球手的距离 ≤ rCatchMaxRadius（400 cm）。之后按「进圈时的球速 × 接球拉回倍率」直线拉到手上（停在 CatchAttachDistance）。进圈前跌到地面以下就判定落地掉球。命中瞬间另加顿帧 0.06 s。

0.7 与 +100 是 RedirectToTeammate 里写死的常量，模拟器里可调，方便试新数值。

6. Weak 节奏

开局追击 U(间隔最短, 间隔最长) → Weak U(基础 − 偏差, 基础 + 偏差) → 追击 → Weak …

Weak 时长来自 BT_AI 的 Weaken 分支（Wait 12 s，随机偏差 3 s，UE 的 BTTask_Wait 在 ±偏差内均匀随机）。两次 Weak 之间的间隔取决于玩家多久能触发声控陷阱，这里按 10–20 s 均匀随机假设。伤害只看命中瞬间是不是 Weak：在 Weak 末尾出手、命中时 Weak 已结束，只扣 1 点。

7. 统计方式

  • 蒙特卡洛：用固定种子跑 N 局（默认 2000）。调参后用同一串随机数重算，对比更稳定。
  • 「理想情况」：命中率和接球率都按 100%，Weak 时长取基础值，追击间隔取中值，跑一局确定结果。
  • 「在第几轮 Weak 击杀」= 击杀时所处的 Weak 轮次；若击杀发生在 Weak 外（飞行中跨出 Weak），记为已结束的 Weak 轮数。

8. 已知简化

  • 没有模拟 Boss 的技能、位移和对玩家的威胁；Weak 期间 Boss 停止移动（AIMoveStop），所以距离按固定值处理。
  • 玩家与 Boss 的距离、受击点高度、手部高度、命中率、接球率都是假设值，需要按关卡实际情况调。
  • 空气阻力在 DA 中为 0，未建模；Perfect Catch 不影响伤害，只给位移，也未建模。
  • BT_AI 有 EnemyHealth ≤ 90 / 60 / 40 的技能分支，只改变追击期行为，不影响伤害，未建模。

1. One throw, step by step

  1. The player holding the orb only throws during a Weak phase. Outside Weak they hold it, and throw once the next Weak starts plus the "Reaction after Weak starts" delay.
  2. The orb flies at the Boss and rolls against the hit rate. On a miss it lands and goes Idle: the streak resets, and the other player continues after the "Pickup time after a drop".
  3. On a hit, damage is resolved by the Boss state at the moment of impact and subtracted from its HP.
  4. The orb auto-rebounds to the other player (RedirectToTeammate). If the rebound physically hits the ground before reaching the catch radius, or the catch roll fails, the orb is dropped and the streak resets.
  5. A catch adds +1 to the streak and starts a 0.75 s throw cooldown; the next throw waits max(cooldown, aim time).

2. Damage

In Weak: damage = BaseOrbDamage × (1 + DamageRatioPerRebound × min(n, nMaxRebound)) × (1 + per-round bonus × (k − 1)) Not Weak: damage = non-Weak damage (currently 1; no streak or round bonus) HP = Clamp(HP − damage, 0, MaxHP); the Boss dies at HP ≤ 0

n is the catch streak (nReboundCount), k is the Weak round number. With "Bonus applies from round 1" checked, (k − 1) becomes k.

Source: MyOrbActor::GetFinalOrbDamage; BP_Enemy "HP Damage" event (uses FinalDamage when EnemyState == Weakened, otherwise 1.0). The per-round bonus is a planned parameter and does not exist in the game yet.

3. Streak n and rebound count R

  • n: +1 on every catch (normal and Perfect catches count the same), capped at nMaxRebound.
  • R: +1 each time the orb hits the Boss, before the rebound speed is computed; uncapped (only limited by max speed).
  • Both reset only when the orb enters the Idle state (SetOrbState(Idle) → ResetRebound). The orb goes Idle when it comes to rest on the ground (missed or not caught), when the catcher becomes invalid during the catch pull, when it respawns after going out of bounds, when a player who falls off the map respawns carrying it, and on a team wipe or checkpoint reset.
  • The end of a Weak phase, how long the orb is held, passing without hitting the Boss, and player damage do not reset it. So with the Weak-only strategy, a streak held at the end of a Weak phase carries into the next one.

Source: DreamCatchersCharacter::ExecuteCatch → IncrementRebound; the Idle branch of MyOrbActor::HandleStateChange; OnProjectileStop, TeleportToRespawn.

4. Throw flight time

throw speed v = base speed × (1 + ThrowSpeedRatioPerRebound × n), capped at BaseMaxSpeed Quick throw: base speed = BaseThrowSpeed, gravity scale = BaseGravityScale Aimed throw: base speed = Lerp(AimThrowSpeed, AimThrowSpeedMax, charge), gravity scale = Lerp(AimThrowGravityScale, AimGravityScaleMax, charge) flight time = d / (v · cos θ), θ = low-arc angle that reaches (d, hit-point height)

Throws in the game do not home in on targets (the code notes auto-aim is not implemented, so Target is always null); launch pitch = crosshair direction + atan(ThrowArcZ). The simulator assumes players aim just right to hit, so it uses the low-arc projectile solution. If the target is beyond max range, the page shows a warning and estimates flight time at 45°.

5. Rebound flight (speeds up with R)

rebound speed = BaseThrowSpeed × 0.7 + R × 100, capped at BaseMaxSpeed pitch = max(pitch toward teammate, MinimumLaunchPitchDegrees) gravity = 980 × this throw's gravity scale (the rebound keeps the throw's gravity)

Integrated per frame (1/240 s) from the Boss hit point until the orb is within rCatchMaxRadius (400 cm) of the catcher. It is then pulled in a straight line at "speed on entering the radius × catch pull multiplier" and attaches at CatchAttachDistance. Falling below ground before entering the radius counts as a drop. A 0.06 s hit stop is added on each hit.

0.7 and +100 are constants hard-coded in RedirectToTeammate; they are adjustable here so new values can be tried.

6. Weak timing

Opening chase U(gap min, gap max) → Weak U(base − deviation, base + deviation) → chase → Weak …

Weak duration comes from the Weaken branch in BT_AI (Wait 12 s with 3 s random deviation; UE's BTTask_Wait samples uniformly within ±deviation). The gap between Weak phases depends on how fast players trigger the sound trap and is assumed uniform in 10–20 s. Damage only checks whether the Boss is Weak at impact: a throw released late in Weak that lands after Weak ends deals 1.

7. Statistics

  • Monte Carlo: N runs (default 2000) with a fixed seed. After a parameter change the same random sequence is reused, so comparisons are stable.
  • "Ideal case": 100% hit and catch rate, Weak at its base duration, gaps at their midpoint; one deterministic run.
  • "Kill round" = the Weak round in progress at the kill; if the kill lands outside Weak (an in-flight throw that crosses the end of Weak), it counts the number of finished Weak rounds.

8. Known simplifications

  • Boss skills, movement and threat to players are not simulated; the Boss stops moving during Weak (AIMoveStop), so distances are fixed.
  • Player-to-Boss distances, hit-point height, hand height, hit rate and catch rate are assumptions; tune them to the actual level.
  • Air drag is 0 in the DA and not modeled; Perfect Catch only gives movement, not damage, and is not modeled.
  • BT_AI has skill branches at EnemyHealth ≤ 90 / 60 / 40; they only change chase behavior, not damage, and are not modeled.
